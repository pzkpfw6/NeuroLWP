# NeuroLWP

An interactive Live wallpaper featuring Neuro-sama and Evil Neuro, built for KLWP (requires KLWP Pro)

## Features:
- Music Controls
- Weather forecasts
- Lots of UI customizations
- Moods
- Lots of costumes and hats
- Custom costumes and hats support
- ...and many more
- ### Plugins
	- Track your bank account balance (for Sber and T-Bank)
	- Track your rides and food delivery 
	- Know when vedal987 is streaming on Twitch 
	- Get vedal987 stream schedule right on your home screen 
	- ...and many more
